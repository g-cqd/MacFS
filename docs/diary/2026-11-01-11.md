# Diary — 2026-11-01 (11)

## What FSKit needs for a writable mount, and FSKit's agent going stale (roadmap 0017)

Probes on the installed ext module, a writable copy of `ext4.img`, every write handler still answering EROFS (the image was
byte-identical after each).

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A mount without `rdonly` is read-write | `mount -F -t ext4` of a writable device; the module logs `isWritable` and the task options | **REFUTED** while the volume asks `requestedMountOptions: .readOnly`: FSKit loaded a writable device, passed no `rdonly`, and mounted read-only. With `[]`, the same mount is read-write (and writes reach the module) |
| macOS automounts a writable image read-write | attach without `-nomount` | **REFUTED**: Disk Arbitration passes `-o …,rdonly` itself; the default stays read-only without anything from us |
| `DADiskMount` mounts a writable device | the DA probe, with and without arguments | **REFUTED**: Disk Arbitration first runs the module's FSKit check, which our modules lack (ENOTSUP), and refuses (0xc05c). The read-only devices of the earlier research needed no check |
| Declaring `FSCheckOptionSyntax` (as Apple's msdos does) and a check on the volume, then the module, makes DA mount it | two installs | **Not established**: the volume's check was never called; after the second, ext mounts failed ("Unable to invoke task") and writable images would not attach. Reverted. FSKit's agent was stale at the time (below), so the probe must be repeated |
| FSKit's view of our modules follows their registration | its own listing (team-signed probe) against pluginkit's | **REFUTED**: after several installs `fskit_agent` (running since 18 October) listed one module, then another, while pluginkit had all four; it pruned the others from the enabled list, and the owner re-enabled them. `launchctl kickstart -k user/<uid>/com.apple.fskit.fskit_agent` restored the full view (SIGTERM is ignored; the job is in the `user` domain, not `gui`) |
| `install --check` passes once the install succeeds | three runs | **REFUTED** for NTFS, twice: "Couldn't communicate with a helper application" while FSKit's SIGTERMed module process lingered after the previous unmount. The check retries that answer once, after 5 s |
