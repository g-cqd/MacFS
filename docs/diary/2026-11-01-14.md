## ext2 allocation: files grow, holes fill (roadmap 0026 step 2)

Two phases per allocating write: the bitmaps, the descriptors' counts, the new data blocks and the new indirect blocks
(written whole, pointers included), then a flush, then the links (existing indirect blocks, the inode). The oracle for an
ext2 crash is what Linux promises: `e2fsck -fp` repairs it (exit 0 or 1), and `e2fsck -fn` after finds nothing.
