|---|---|---|

| The zstd CLI is the oracle for damaged frames | 2,400 damaged frames | **REFUTED**: its streaming decoder accepts a frame shorter than its declared size and its memory limit refuses large windows. libzstd's one-shot `ZSTD_decompress` into a flat buffer is the oracle, as MacFS decodes |
